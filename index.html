Questions tradies ask

I've already got a Facebook page. Do I need this?

Facebook is where you post. A website is where people check you're legit before they ring. Google shows websites, not Facebook pages, when someone searches "electrician Logan". Keep Facebook, add the site.

Do I have to write anything?

No. We ask you ten minutes of questions and write it all. You read it, tell us what's wrong, we fix it.

What if I want changes later?

Small changes in the first month are free. After that, $50 for a batch of changes, or we show you how to do them yourself.

Why so cheap?

We're new in Brisbane and want five good sites we can show people. That's the founding rate. Once we have them, the price goes up.

What happens if you disappear?

Nothing. The domain, hosting and site files are all in your name. Anyone can take over from where we left off.
